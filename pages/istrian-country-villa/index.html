Villa Afrika – Svetvinčenat, Istria

A stunning villa tucked away in the countryside of Svetvinčenat, Istria. This is the ultimate destination to recover from the stress of city life. Come and relax at our beautiful villa.

Istria is incredible, its natural beauty alongside the stunning Adriatic Sea, it is a bit of Italy combined with all the stunning Croatian hospitality experience.

Villa Afrika is a 4-bedroom countryside villa with elegant interiors and a private pool, surrounded by nature yet only a 15-minute drive from the Adriatic coast.

The villa

  • Sleeps 8 guests
  • 4 bedrooms with king-size beds and air conditioning, 4 bathrooms
  • 50 m² private pool and gardens, outdoor shower
  • Private parking, Wi-Fi, 5 TVs, BBQ
  • Location: Butkovići, Svetvinčenat, Istria, Croatia
  • Check-in from 17:00, check-out by 10:00
