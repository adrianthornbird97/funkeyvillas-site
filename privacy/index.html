Privacy Statement

Effective Date: November 2026

This Privacy Statement applies to www.funkeyvillas.com owned and operated by Funkey Villas. This Privacy Statement describes how we collect and use the information, which may include personal data, you provide on our website: www.funkeyvillas.com. It also describes the choices available to you regarding our use of your personal data and how you can access and update this data.

Data Collection

The types of personal data that we collect include:

Your first name, last name, email address, phone number, and home address;

Payment details, which you enter on the secure pages of our booking partners (NightsBridge or Lodgify) and which they process;

Guest stay data, including date of arrival and departure, special requests made, observations about your service preferences (including room preferences, facilities, or any other services used);

Data you provide regarding your marketing preferences or in the course of participating in surveys, contests, or promotional offers;

You may always choose what personal data (if any) you want to provide to us. However, if you choose not to provide certain details, some of your transactions with us may be impacted.

Data We Collect Automatically

When using our website, we also collect information automatically, some of which may be personal data. This includes data such as language settings, IP address, location, device settings, device OS, log information, time of usage, URL requested, status report, user agent (information about the browser version), operating system, result (viewer or booker), browsing history, user Booking ID, and type of data viewed. We may also collect data automatically through cookies. For information on how we use cookies, click here.

Processing Purposes

We use your personal data for the following purposes:

A. Reservations: We use your personal data to complete and administer your online reservation.

B. Customer Service: We use your personal data to provide customer service.

C.Booking partners

: Online reservations for our Camps Bay villas are made through NightsBridge (book.nightsbridge.com) and for Villa Afrika in Istria through Lodgify (checkout.lodgify.com). When you book, the details you enter on their secure booking pages, including your name, contact details, payment details and stay preferences, are processed by them so that your reservation can be completed. Their own privacy policies also apply.

Enquiry form

: Messages sent with the enquiry form on our website are delivered to our email inbox by FormSubmit (formsubmit.co). This includes the name, email address, phone number, dates and message you enter.

Newsletter

: If you subscribe to our newsletter, your email address and name are stored by Mailchimp (Intuit Mailchimp), which sends our newsletters. Every newsletter includes an unsubscribe link.

WhatsApp

: If you contact us by WhatsApp, your messages are handled by WhatsApp (Meta) under its own privacy policy.

Website hosting

: Our website is hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.), which may log technical data such as your IP address when you visit, for security and operation of the service. The map on our website is provided by OpenStreetMap.
