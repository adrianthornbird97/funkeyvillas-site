About this villa

Three-bedroom Villa with Private Pool

Stunning, excellently located & serviced Camps Bay villa with private entrance, just above Bakoven Beach.

This villa features ocean and mountain views, a private pool, balcony, patio with BBQ, indoor and outdoor sitting and dining areas. Fully fitted kitchen with dishwasher, washing machine and dryer.

The main bedrooms have king-size beds, and towels and linen are provided.

Situated a short walk from Hussar Grill and Bootleggers.

Upper floors accessible by stairs only.
